DoDo is a privacy-friendly, offline-first productivity app that combines To-Do lists, Spaced Repetition learning, and a personal Diary.

Features:
* Smart To-Do List with priorities and time tracking.
* Free Time Tracker that automatically calculates free time.
* Spaced Repetition System for effective learning/revision.
* Personal Diary for daily notes.
* Home Screen Widget for quick access.

Privacy:
The app is completely offline-first. Cloud Sync is strictly optional, and users can use their own custom hosting/server. It uses AES-256 Encryption so no one can read the data without the key.
